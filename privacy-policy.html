Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how Starfern Games ("we") handles information in our mobile games, including Orbit Hop. If you have any questions, contact us at starferngames@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, coins, upgrades, skins and settings) is stored only on your device. We don't run our own servers, and we don't ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising and consent

Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent message. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling a game deletes all progress stored on your device. Data collected by the Google services above is retained according to Google's policies; analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. To request deletion of any data associated with your device, email us at starferngames@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
